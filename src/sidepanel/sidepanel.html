Local settings

These settings are stored in Chrome extension storage. The extension does not call an LLM or external API.

How to use GEO Lens

  1. Open any normal website page.
  2. Click the GEO Lens extension icon.
  3. Review the grades, highlighted evidence, and prioritized fixes.
  4. Click Copy LLM prompt and paste it into your preferred AI tool.

Chrome blocks extensions from auditing internal pages such as chrome://, the Chrome Web Store, browser settings pages, and some PDFs.

Grade meaning

A/A+ means the section is clear, answerable, entity-rich, and evidence-ready. C usually means useful but generic. D/F means it is likely too vague, thin, unsupported, or hard for an answer engine to extract.
